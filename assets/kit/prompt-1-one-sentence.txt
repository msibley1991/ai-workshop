Build a one-page website for a design workshop called "How (Did You [Make] This?)". It's part of NMD November, a month of workshops at RIT Croatia in Zagreb. The workshop teaches design students how to use AI as a tool in their design process, by building a website step by step. It takes place on Thursday 26 November, 15:30–18:00, in Lab 3, and is led by Nora Miskulin and Maxwell Sibley. It's free and open to beginners. The page should explain what the workshop is about, who it's for and what people will make, and let visitors sign up with a simple form.
